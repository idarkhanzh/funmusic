How to play

Your left hand chooses which chord. Your right hand chooses how it sounds. Everything is computed from 21 hand landmarks in the browser — no video is uploaded anywhere.

Left hand — chord selection

"Inward" means tilting toward the centre of the frame — mirrored per hand, so both hands lean toward each other.

Right hand — voicing & expression

Right hand above leftLouder
Right hand below leftQuieter

Major or minor always comes from the left hand — the right hand only decides how that chord is voiced. Counting ignores the thumb, which leaves it free to switch octaves at any finger count.

Volume is the height of your right hand relative to your left, not its position in frame: level hands sit at about half volume, and a quarter of the frame in either direction covers the full range. With only the right hand visible it falls back to absolute height so the instrument stays playable.

If it feels reversed

Handedness comes from the tracker and depends on your camera. If the left-hand panel is reacting to your right hand, press Swap hands in the control row.
